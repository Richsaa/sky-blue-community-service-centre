How Sky Blue connects your organisation

Different organisations use different workflows, but the platform follows the same simple principle.

1

Someone starts

A customer, parent, employee, resident, branch or member starts a request.

2

Sky Blue guides them

The correct questions, options, forms, pictures or workflow are presented.

3

Organisation receives it

The request reaches the correct business, teacher, department, branch or administrator.

4

Management acts

Staff and management can process requests and communicate updates through the platform.

Sky Blue Community Service Centre — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 22 September 2026

1. Introduction

Sky Blue Community Service Centre is a digital community service reporting platform operated by Sky Blue Solution. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect information submitted through our platform, including WhatsApp and web-based reporting services.

2. Information We Collect

When you submit a community service report, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Your telephone or WhatsApp number
  • Your location or address
  • The description of the reported issue
  • The category of the issue
  • Messages and information provided during WhatsApp conversations
  • Your report reference number and related status information

3. How We Use Information

We use the information submitted to:

  • Receive and register community service reports
  • Create report reference numbers
  • Track and manage reported issues
  • Communicate with residents about their reports
  • Route reports to the relevant department, service provider or responsible authority
  • Provide updates about reported issues
  • Maintain records and improve the service

4. WhatsApp Communications

Where WhatsApp is used to submit or receive reports, messages and related information may be processed for community service reporting and communication. WhatsApp and Meta may also process information in accordance with their own terms and privacy policies.

5. Sharing of Information

Information may be shared where reasonably necessary with the relevant municipal, ward, service, operational or responsible authority for the purpose of addressing a reported community service issue.

We do not sell residents' personal information.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure.

7. Data Retention

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to manage reports, provide services, maintain operational records and comply with applicable legal requirements.

8. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request information about personal data held about you and may request correction of inaccurate information.

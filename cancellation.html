1. Cancelling a Subscription

Customers may request cancellation of a recurring subscription before the next scheduled billing date.

Cancellation is intended to prevent future recurring subscription charges once the cancellation has been successfully processed.

2. Current Subscription Period

Unless otherwise required by applicable law, cancelling a subscription does not automatically terminate access immediately.

Where applicable, the customer may continue to access the service until the end of the subscription period that has already been paid for.

Important: To reduce the possibility of another recurring charge, customers should submit cancellation requests before their next scheduled billing date.

3. How to Request Cancellation

Customers may request cancellation using the available account cancellation mechanism or by contacting Sky Blue Solution.

Cancellation requests sent by email should include:

  • Customer or business name.
  • Account or subscription information.
  • Email address associated with the account.
  • Any available payment or subscription reference.

4. Recurring Payments

If a cancellation request is received and successfully processed before the next scheduled recurring payment, reasonable steps will be taken to prevent that future charge.

A payment that has already been successfully processed before cancellation may not automatically be refundable.

Refund requests are handled separately under our Refund Policy.

5. Failed Payments

If a recurring payment fails, access to the service may be restricted or suspended until the outstanding subscription payment is resolved.

6. Termination for Misuse

Sky Blue Solution may suspend or terminate an account where reasonably necessary because of unlawful activity, fraud, abuse, serious security risks or a material breach of our Terms & Conditions.

7. Consumer Rights

Nothing in this Cancellation Policy is intended to remove or restrict any consumer right or legal remedy that cannot lawfully be excluded under applicable South African law.
